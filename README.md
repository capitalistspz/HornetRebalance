# Rebalance

A Hollow Knight: Silksong mod to rebalance Hornet as suggested in [Holland](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCnVGHE75UsCcjCN2DlIOJLg)'s videos.

Disclaimer: The full extent of my affiliation with Holland is as a regular viewer.

## Changes

### Clawline
- No longer requires or consumes silk
### Hunter
- Down attack delay reduced
- Taking damage with fully evolved Hunter's crest only drops focus down by the amount of hits required for focus 1 (6 points)

### Tools
#### Shard Pendant
- Most bosses drop (100) shards upon death (may need testing)
#### Weighted Belt
- No more attack recoil
#### Wreath of Purity
- Now yellow
- Lasts forever
#### Thief's Mark
- Drop chance of each rosary type increased (+ 16.6...%)
- Loss chance fixed to 2%
- Loss cap is fixed to 10 rosaries
#### Magma Bell
- Now yellow
#### Dead Bug's Purse
- Cocoon gives back all lost rosaries
#### Magnetite Brooch
- Attract shards too
- Collect when exiting scene
- ~~Precent drops from being destroyed by spikes, and attract them early~~ (having trouble with this)
### Silkspeed Anklets
- No longer requires or consumes silk
### Ascendant's Grip
- Increases attack range while on wall
